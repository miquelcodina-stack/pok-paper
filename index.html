The asset Pokédex

Auto: uses Sword & Shield for forms with an archived render, then another available game-version render, Pokémon HOME, official artwork, or archived pixels. Game source lists only sources archived for the exact selected form. Pick a specific game or keep Auto. Changing to a form without that game resets the source to Auto; unavailable images are never replaced with a different form.

Sword & Shield: animated renders by FallenDeity from original game models, distributed through PokéAPI’s sprite archive. These are community-produced renders of game assets, not an official Nintendo wallpaper collection. Pokémon introduced after those games do not have Sword & Shield assets.

Other games: archived game-model renders include Scarlet & Violet, Legends: Arceus, Legends: Z-A, Brilliant Diamond & Shining Pearl, Ultra Sun & Ultra Moon, Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire, X & Y, and Champions where that exact form has a source. These are archive-supplied images of game models, not a claim of an official Nintendo render collection. The active source is credited below the preview; available animations, shiny colors, views, and gender variants follow the chosen game's assets.

Showdown animated: high-quality animated battle sprites from the Pokémon Showdown collection, served from PokéAPI. Front/back and shiny variants are available where archived; some cosmetic forms use the base species sprite. Classic pixels: original animated Generation V game sprites where available. Later-generation and alternate-form pixel sprites may be community-made and are labeled as archived sprites. Official artwork: illustrations from the PokéAPI archive. Regional, Mega, Gigantamax and separately archived cosmetic forms are included, such as Arceus types, Vivillon patterns and Alcremie decorations. Forms without an image in a particular source remain unavailable in that source.

All Pokémon names, characters, artwork, models, and sprites belong to Nintendo, Game Freak, and The Pokémon Company. This fan project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Please use wallpapers for personal enjoyment.

A quick field guide

Combine generation and type filters with name or National Pokédex number search. Surprise me and R pick within your current filters; / focuses search and Space pauses motion. Turn on Move below the preview to drag with mouse or touch; arrow keys nudge while the preview is focused. Turn off Pokémon shadow to remove the added ground shadow.

Your Pokémon: Add Pokémon lets you choose another sprite from the Pokédex. The picker normally replaces only the selected sprite. Duplicate copies its settings; Delete removes it and Undo delete restores it. Select a sprite chip to edit its own form, colors, source, size, animation, shadow and position. Move mode also lets you select and drag sprites on the canvas; the last sprite in the list is drawn on top.

PNG saves the entire scene. GIF includes every enabled animated sprite, or a still scene when none are animated. A single animation exports its complete loop. With multiple animations, the export spans the longest source loop and repeats shorter animations within it; different loop lengths may not all line up at the repeat boundary. Max quality retains full resolution and every frame-change event, using 256 colors per output frame and dithering. GIF timing has 10 ms precision. Bigger files take longer; Cancel stops encoding without changing your design. Exports include all sprite positions and shadows, without editor guides.

GIF encoding uses gifenc (MIT license). Adaptive colors blend the palettes sampled from all ready sprites in your scene. Your design and exports stay in this tab and are not uploaded.
